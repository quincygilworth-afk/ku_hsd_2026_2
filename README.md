This project depicts Gunter's Blacksmith shop where he sells goods such as swords, platr amors, chain mail, and cooking pots. In this application you have the ability to purchase said items and they will be put on his to-do list for what should be made. Finally, if you purchase 5 items, in which you will recieve a coupon. 


For each item, you have the ability to push the button that lets you buy one of that specific item. Once purchased, it is put on the list below that represents Gunter's to-do list. 

The game on page two is called: "Gunter's Game". It is a clicking game where you need to click the piece of rock(button). When pushed the number of clicks left will go down. As you click the piece of stone, you will gain various ores such as coal, iron, diamonds, gold, emeralds, and radium. Once the number reaches 0, you will be rewarded with radium. It prevents you from mining on because radium is radioactive and can kill you. That science fact transitions the game to an ending.
